About

Research at the intersection of population, politics, and power.

I am an Assistant Professor at La Sierra University. My research interests span international relations, political demography and geography, and political economy. My work examines population dynamics and their political implications, including migration, demographic transition, economic development, state capacity, conflict and cooperation, and changing patterns of international power.

Research

Research Areas

Political Demography & Geography

Population change, migration, aging, demographic transition, political stability, and the geographical dimensions of political and economic development.

International Relations

Conflict and cooperation, international power dynamics, political capacity, power transition, and changes in the international system.

Political Economy

Economic growth, migration and development, state capacity, institutions, technological change, and their political consequences.

Migration & Development

The relationship between migration, labor mobility, demographic structure, and economic development at national and subnational levels.

Population & Political Stability

How demographic transitions, aging societies, and changing population structures affect political capacity and domestic and international stability.

U.S.–China Relations

Power dynamics, technological competition and cooperation, artificial intelligence, political economy, and the evolving international order.
